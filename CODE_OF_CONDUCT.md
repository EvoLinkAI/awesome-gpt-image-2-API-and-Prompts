# Code of Conduct

## Our standard

We want this project to be a welcoming place for people sharing prompts, documentation, corrections, and localization work.

Contributors and maintainers are expected to:

- communicate respectfully and focus feedback on the work;
- credit creators and correct attribution mistakes promptly;
- respect privacy, consent, intellectual property, and platform rules;
- avoid harassment, discrimination, threats, sexualized attention, spam, and deliberate disruption;
- avoid publishing private information, credentials, or internal review material.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit or remove contributions, comments, issues, or other participation that does not meet this standard. Repeated or serious violations may result in temporary or permanent participation restrictions.

To report conduct concerns privately, email `support@evolink.ai` with the subject prefix `[Code of Conduct]`. Include links and enough context for maintainers to review the report. Do not retaliate against anyone who raises a concern in good faith.

Maintainers should protect the privacy of reporters as far as reasonably possible and disclose conflicts of interest before handling a report.

## Scope

This policy applies in repository spaces and when someone is representing this project in public community spaces.
